Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit fix.dataspecshub.com (“the site”), how it's used, and the choices you have.

Who runs this site

The site is a personal project run by Dima, an individual. For anything in this policy, contact fixcar.dimas@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, when you visit we and our service providers may receive your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and time spent. We don't ask you to create an account or collect payment details.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. This site uses:

  • Necessary cookies to remember your cookie choice.
  • Analytics cookies from Google Analytics 4, which help us understand which guides are useful. These only load if you accept them.
  • Advertising cookies from third parties, if and when ads are shown on the site (see below).

You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

We use Google Analytics 4 to see aggregated information about visits. Google may set cookies and process data under its own privacy policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. More information is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We don't sell your personal information for money.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. This includes rights under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, and under US state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), as well as similar laws in Canada and Australia. California residents may also opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context advertising. To make a request, email fixcar.dimas@gmail.com.

We keep emails only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record keeping. Analytics data is kept according to our Google Analytics retention settings. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but the site is served over HTTPS.

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.
