Rough Idle: Causes and What to Check First

By Dima · Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 5, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

A rough idle most often comes from worn spark plugs or coils, a vacuum leak, a dirty air filter or throttle body, or a faulty sensor. Start with the cheap checks: read any stored codes, inspect the air filter and look for loose or cracked hoses. If the check engine light is flashing, avoid driving hard and get it checked soon.

What is a rough idle?

A rough idle is when the engine shakes, vibrates or the revs hunt up and down while the car is stopped. It may feel worse at traffic lights, in gear or when the engine is cold. It's a symptom, not a diagnosis, so the aim is to narrow down the cause.

What to check first

  1. Look at the dashboard. A steady or flashing check engine light is a clue. See Check Engine Light: Flashing vs Steady.
  2. Read the stored code with an OBD-II reader. Codes such as P0300 to P0304 point to misfires.
  3. Check the air filter and the intake hoses for cracks or loose clamps.
  4. Listen for hissing near the intake, which can indicate a vacuum leak.
  5. Check the battery and the charging system. A low voltage can affect idle.
  6. Make sure the gas cap is tight and you haven't just used poor-quality fuel.

Safety note. Keep hands, hair and clothing away from belts and fans when the engine is running. Don't spray flammable cleaners near a hot or running engine.

Cold idle vs warm idle

  • Rough only when cold: often sensors, injectors or a vacuum leak.
  • Rough only when warm: may be a sensor, ignition or fuel problem.
  • Rough in gear but smooth in Park: often a worn engine mount.
  • Rough with the air conditioning on: idle control or a weak engine, or a drive belt issue. See Squealing Belt Noise.

When to see a mechanic

Get the car checked if the light is flashing, the idle doesn't improve after basic checks, the engine stalls, or you notice smoke. See Exhaust Smoke Colors Explained for what the color can tell you. Regular servicing, including plugs, filters and oil, helps prevent many causes. See How Often Should You Change Your Engine Oil?

FAQ

Why does my car shake at idle but drive fine?

Possible causes include worn mounts, a mild misfire or a dirty throttle body. A scan can help narrow it down.

Can bad spark plugs cause a rough idle?

Yes, worn plugs and failing coils are among the most common causes.

Can a dirty air filter cause a rough idle?

It can contribute, especially if the filter is very clogged, though other causes are more common.

Is a rough idle dangerous?

Not always, but a flashing check engine light, stalling or smoke means you should stop driving and get help.

Why does my idle go up and down?

A vacuum leak, a dirty throttle body or an idle control fault can make the revs hunt.
