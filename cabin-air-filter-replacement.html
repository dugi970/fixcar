Cabin Air Filter: Signs You Need a New One and How to Swap It

By Dima · Published November 1, 2026 · Updated November 5, 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer

Replace the cabin air filter when airflow from the vents is weak, the car smells musty or the windows fog up and clear slowly. Many makers suggest roughly once a year or every 12,000 to 15,000 miles. It usually sits behind the glovebox or in the engine bay near the windscreen, and the new one must go in with the airflow arrow pointing the right way.

The cabin air filter is one of the cheapest parts on your car and one of the easiest to forget. If your vents blow weakly or smell stale, it's usually the first thing worth checking.

The cabin air filter is one of the most neglected parts on a car. When the airflow from the vents gets weak or the car starts to smell stale, I check it before looking for anything more complicated. It's a simple part, but replacing a badly clogged one makes a noticeable difference straight away.

What it does

The cabin filter cleans the air that comes into the car through the heating, ventilation and air conditioning system. It catches dust, pollen, leaves and other debris before they reach you and the blower. Some filters also have an activated carbon layer to cut down on odours.

Signs it needs replacing

  • Weak airflow from the vents, even with the fan on high
  • A musty or stale smell when the fan or air conditioning starts
  • Windows that fog up and clear slowly
  • A louder blower that seems to be working hard
  • More dust on the dashboard, or worse allergies in the car

Pull the filter out and look. If it's grey or black, packed with leaves or flattened, it's done.

How often to change it

Many manufacturers suggest roughly once a year or every 12,000 to 15,000 miles, but check your service schedule. If you drive in dusty areas, on dirt roads or under lots of trees, check it more often.

The engine has its own filter, which does a different job. See Engine Air Filter: Dirty Symptoms and How to Change It. Oil filters are covered in Oil Filter: How Often to Change It.

Which filter to buy

Standard (particulate) filter

Catches dust and pollen. Fine for most drivers and the cheapest option.

Activated carbon filter

Adds a carbon layer that helps with odours and exhaust smells in traffic. Usually costs a little more.

Whichever you choose, match it to your exact model and year. Filters that look similar can differ in size.

How to change it

The location depends on the car. On many cars it sits behind the glovebox. On others, including several BMW models, it's in a housing in the engine bay near the base of the windscreen. Your manual or a quick search for your model will tell you.

  1. Locate the filter housing and open the cover. This may mean emptying and unclipping the glovebox, or lifting off a plastic cover.
  2. Slide out the old filter and note which way the airflow arrow points.
  3. Clean out leaves and debris from the housing. A vacuum cleaner or a brush works well.
  4. Slide in the new filter with the arrow pointing the same way as the old one.
  5. Refit the cover, check the clips are properly seated and reattach the glovebox.
  6. Turn the fan on to check airflow and listen for rattles.

Tip. Take a photo of the old filter in place before you remove it. It makes it easy to check the direction of the arrow.

Still smells bad after a new filter?

A musty smell that stays can come from moisture in the air conditioning evaporator. Running the fan with the AC off for the last couple of minutes of a drive helps it dry out. If the smell persists, a workshop can clean the system.

While the glovebox is open, it's a good moment for other quick jobs, such as replacing worn wiper blades.

Common mistakes

  • Fitting the filter backwards
  • Forcing a filter that's the wrong size
  • Leaving the cover loose, so unfiltered air gets in
  • Forgetting it entirely for years

FAQ

How often should I change the cabin air filter?

Roughly once a year or every 12,000 to 15,000 miles, but check your service schedule. Dusty roads or lots of leaves mean checking more often.

Why does my car smell musty when the fan is on?

A clogged filter or moisture in the air conditioning evaporator. Replace the filter first, then dry the system by running the fan with the AC off at the end of a trip.

Can I clean and reuse a cabin air filter?

Standard paper filters aren't meant to be washed. You can tap off loose debris, but replacing it is cheap and works better.

Does a dirty cabin filter affect the air conditioning?

It restricts airflow, so the system feels weaker and the blower has to work harder.
