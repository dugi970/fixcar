Steering Wheel Shakes at Highway Speed: Causes and Fixes

By Dima · Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 5, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

A steering wheel that shakes at around 80 to 110 km/h (50 to 70 mph) is most often caused by unbalanced front wheels. Other causes include a bent wheel, uneven tyre wear, loose wheel nuts, worn suspension parts and warped brake discs. Check tyre pressures and wheel nut tightness first, then have the wheels balanced.

Why does the steering wheel shake?

Anything that spins at speed and isn't perfectly round or balanced can create a vibration that travels up the steering column. The speed at which it happens, and whether it's present during braking, helps pinpoint the cause.

Safety note. If the shaking starts suddenly, the car wobbles badly or you hear knocking, slow down and stop safely. A loose wheel is an emergency.

What to check first

  1. Check the tyre pressures with a gauge. See How to Check Tire Pressure Correctly.
  2. Look at each tyre for bulges, cuts, flat spots and uneven wear.
  3. Check that all wheel nuts are present and tight, to the torque in your manual.
  4. Check whether the vibration changes when you brake, accelerate or coast.
  5. Note the speed range. If it appears and fades at a specific speed, it's likely balance.

Wheel balancing vs alignment

Balancing adds small weights so the wheel and tyre spin evenly. It fixes vibration. Alignment sets the angles of the wheels so the car tracks straight and tyres wear evenly. It fixes pulling and uneven wear. They are different jobs, and sometimes you need both, particularly after new tyres, hitting a kerb or a pothole.

When to get professional help

If balancing doesn't cure it, a workshop can check for a bent wheel or worn steering and suspension components. Don't keep driving at speed with a vibration that is getting worse. If you've had a puncture recently, see How to Change a Flat Tire Safely for what to check after the repair.

FAQ

Why does my steering wheel shake only at highway speed?

Usually wheel imbalance, which shows up at certain speeds. Rebalancing the wheels often cures it.

Can low tyre pressure cause steering wheel vibration?

It can add to wobble and uneven wear, so check pressures first.

Is it safe to drive with a shaking steering wheel?

A mild vibration is usually not an emergency, but get it checked. Sudden or severe shaking means stop.

Why does it shake when I brake?

Often uneven or warped brake discs, or worn pads.

How much does balancing cost?

It's one of the cheaper workshop jobs, but prices vary by location. Ask for a quote.
