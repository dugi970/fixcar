Fluid Leaking Under Your Car: What Each Colour Means

By Dima · Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 5, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

Clear, odourless water under the front passenger side in warm weather is usually air-conditioning condensation and is normal. Brown or black oily fluid is engine oil, green, orange or pink sweet-smelling fluid is coolant, and red or brown thin fluid is often transmission or power steering fluid. A slippery clear to yellow-brown leak near a wheel can be brake fluid and is urgent.

How to identify a fluid leak

  1. Slide a clean piece of white cardboard under the car overnight.
  2. Note the colour, thickness and smell of the drips.
  3. Check where the stain is, since the location points to the source.
  4. Look at the fluid levels once the engine is cool.
  5. Take a photo to show a mechanic.

Safety note. A brake fluid leak or a soft pedal is an emergency. Don't drive the car. Coolant is toxic to pets, so clean up spills promptly.

Where the leak is located

  • Front of the engine: oil, coolant or power steering
  • Under the middle of the car: transmission fluid or oil
  • Near a wheel: brake fluid or a failed seal
  • Under the rear: differential or fuel (rare, but serious)

Engine oil leaks

Common sources are the valve cover gasket, sump gasket, drain plug and oil filter. If you've just done an oil change, check the plug and filter first. See How to Change Engine Oil at Home and Burning Smell After an Oil Change. Monitor the level with How to Read an Oil Dipstick.

Coolant leaks

Hoses, the radiator, the water pump and the expansion tank are common culprits. A falling level can lead to overheating, so top up with the correct coolant and fix the source. See How to Check Coolant Level and Car Overheating: Causes and What to Do.

Brake fluid leaks

Check the reservoir level and the area around each wheel. A soft or sinking pedal means don't drive. See Spongy Brake Pedal and How Often to Change Brake Fluid.

What to do next

  • Top up fluids only with the correct type
  • Fix small leaks before they grow
  • Don't ignore fresh puddles of brake fluid or coolant
  • Get a mechanic to diagnose leaks you can't trace

FAQ

Is clear fluid under my car normal?

If it's water from the air conditioning, yes. If it's slippery or smells, it could be brake fluid or another leak.

Is a small oil drip a problem?

A small drip isn't an emergency, but check the level and fix the source before it worsens.

What colour is coolant?

Usually green, orange, pink or blue. It often has a sweet smell.

How do I tell oil from brake fluid?

Brake fluid is thinner, usually clear to light brown and found near a wheel. Oil is usually darker and under the engine.

Can I drive with a leak?

Depends on the fluid. Brake or coolant leaks should not be driven on. Small oil seeps can be monitored.
