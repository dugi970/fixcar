About Fix Car

I'm Dima. I work at a BMW service, and I started Fix Car to write down what I learn: how jobs actually go, where they go wrong and what to watch out for.

I'm still learning the trade, so I'm more of a trainee than a senior mechanic. Over the last two years I've been doing routine services myself, including brakes and shock absorbers. At home I drive a Škoda, so I also look after the everyday maintenance on my own car.

I'm not a master technician and I don't pretend to be. The guides are written from the point of view of someone who is still learning: I explain what I understand, say clearly when I'm unsure and point you to the manufacturer's manual for exact specs. The aim is a clear explanation and an honest warning before you start.

What you'll find here

Practical guides on brakes, oil and fluids, filters, batteries and everyday diagnostics. Topics are the kind of routine jobs I come across at a BMW service and on my own car. The principles apply to most cars, and I flag anything that is model-specific.

How I write

  • Where a job needs tools or parts, the guide lists them before the first step.
  • Where safety matters (and with cars it often does) I add a clear safety note instead of burying it.
  • If I'm not sure about something, I say so and point you to the service manual.
  • When I get something wrong on a job, I write it down, because those are the useful bits.
  • Nothing is copied from other sites. Photos are my own or from free-licence libraries.
  • I don't publish customer details, vehicle identification numbers or internal workshop documents.
