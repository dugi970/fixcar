About Fix Car

I'm Dima, a car owner who got tired of paying for jobs I could do myself. Fix Car is where I write down how those jobs go.

I started with the basics on my own BMW: oil changes, filters, then brakes. Pads and discs turned out to be one of the most satisfying jobs to do at home, and also the one where small mistakes matter most. Since then I've kept doing most of the maintenance myself and keeping notes on what worked.

I'm not a professional mechanic and this isn't a workshop. It's one person's garage, a decent set of tools and a lot of trial and error. That's actually the point: the guides are written for people at the same stage, who want a clear explanation and an honest warning before they start.

What you'll find here

Practical guides on brakes, oil and fluids, filters, batteries and everyday diagnostics. Many are based on jobs I've done on my own BMW, but the principles apply to most cars, and I flag anything that is model-specific.

How I write

  • Where a job needs tools or parts, the guide lists them before the first step.
  • Where safety matters (and with cars it often does) I add a clear safety note instead of burying it.
  • If I'm not sure about something, I say so and point you to the service manual.
  • When I get something wrong on a job, I write it down, because those are the useful bits.
  • Nothing is copied from other sites. Photos are my own or from free-licence libraries.
